01 — What is Touch Lab

触觉，是最先抵达的语言。 Touch is the first language — felt before it is seen.

Touch Lab 触觉实验室是一场关于感知的实验性展览。我们把皮肤当作界面，把温度、压力与震动当作语法——邀请你伸出手，重新阅读这个世界。

An experimental exhibition on perception, by the Visual Communication Department. Skin becomes the interface; heat, pressure and vibration become the grammar. Read the world again — with your hands.
